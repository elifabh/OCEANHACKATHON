What is this habitat worth?

Fisheries support — annual

Velvet crab (Necora puber)

±1 SD . Landings value × kelp-dependency 0.43 (Piñeiro-Corbeira et al. 2025). High confidence.

European lobster (Homarus gammarus)

GEAK low band — expert judgement with written justification; the species is absent from the published dependency set.

Fisheries subtotal

The only subtotal on this panel. Values for different service types are never summed — that would double-count.

Rock-armour replacement rate, Ireland

OPW-funded schemes, 2024–2026 (range ). Shown as a counterfactual illustration — not a value: replacement cost requires demonstrated demand (Shabman & Batie), and that test fails here.

Modelled wave attenuation by the bed height / energy

Field coefficients for subtidal L. hyperborea on exposed Atlantic coasts (Dubi & Tørum 1996; Mork 1996; Løvås & Tørum 2001), applied along four wave axes. Physical effect, shown without a euro figure.
